This final internship project integrates data cleaning, visualization, statistical analysis, and predictive modeling using fresh weather information collected on 6 October 2026 for Bhopal, Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Kolkata. Current temperature, humidity, wind speed, AQI, and multi-day forecast values were organized into reproducible CSV files. The data was cleaned by checking duplicates, missing values, data types, city labels, and dates. Exploratory charts were used to compare current conditions and forecast trends. Pearson correlation and one-way ANOVA were applied to examine relationships and city-level differences. A multiple linear regression model was developed to predict daily maximum temperature from minimum temperature and city. A time-based holdout was used to evaluate the latest observations as unseen data. The model achieved an MAE of approximately 1.10°C, RMSE of 1.33°C, and R² of 0.711. The project demonstrates how live data can support practical, transparent, and data-driven decision making.
